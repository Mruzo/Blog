Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2025

1. Introduction

At MisterUzo, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit misteruzo.com and use our services, including our 3D comic platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and other contact details you provide
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our website and services
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, and device identifiers
  • Cookies: Small data files stored on your device (see our Cookie Policy)
  • Traffic Source Data: Information about how you discovered our website (search engines, social media links, direct visits)
  • Content Sharing: Information about which share options you use to share our content
  • Content Engagement: Which episodes and content you view, comment on, or interact with
3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Process your transactions
  • Send you updates and marketing communications
  • Improve our website and services
  • Protect against fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Understand how users discover our content to improve our website
  • Learn which content resonates most to create better 3D comic episodes
  • See which share options are most helpful to improve user experience
  • Generate insights to help us create better content
4. Understanding User Interactions

We collect information to better understand how users interact with our content:

  • Discovery Patterns: We learn how users find our website to improve our reach
  • Content Preferences: We understand which episodes and content users enjoy most
  • Sharing Behavior: We see which share options users find most helpful
  • User Experience: We learn how users interact with our 3D comic content

Note: This information helps us create better content and improve your experience. All data is stored locally and does not contain personally identifiable information. We do not use third-party services that could access your personal data.

5. Data Storage and Retention

We store different types of data for different periods:

  • Account Information: Retained while your account is active
  • Comments and Interactions: Retained as long as the content exists
  • User Interaction Data: Stored locally to help us improve our content and services
  • Discovery Data: Stored locally for up to 2 years to understand how users find our content
6. Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist in our operations
  • Legal authorities when required by law
  • Business partners with your consent

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. User interaction data is used internally only to improve our content and is not shared with external parties.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure data storage and transmission
  • Local storage of user interaction data to minimize external exposure
8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access Your Data: Request a copy of all personal data we have about you
  • Correct Your Data: Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Delete Your Data: Request deletion of all your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object to Processing: Request we stop processing your data
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise these rights: Contact us at reachout@misteruzo.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to data processing
  • Withdraw consent
  • Data portability
  • Opt out of user interaction data collection by contacting us
9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new policy on our website
  • Updating the "Last updated" date
  • Sending an email notification for significant changes
12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to opt out of user interaction data collection, please contact us at:

  • Email: reachout@misteruzo.com
  • Through our contact form
